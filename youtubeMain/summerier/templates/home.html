What this project does

YouTube Summarizer takes any public YouTube video and generates a concise, structured AI summary of its content — in the format and language you choose. It also analyzes the sentiment of the video's transcript so you know the overall tone before you even watch it.

🧠

AI-Generated Summaries

Powered by Gemini, summaries are generated directly from the video's transcript — standard notes, detailed breakdowns, code walkthroughs, bullet points, or mind maps.

🌐

Multi-Language Output

Get your summary translated into English, Spanish, French, German, or Hindi — pick your preferred language before generating.

💬

Sentiment Analysis

See the overall tone of the video and a sentence-by-sentence sentiment breakdown, color-coded as positive, neutral, or negative.

📊

Personal Dashboard

Every summary you generate is saved to your dashboard with video stats — views, likes, duration, and channel — so you can revisit it anytime.

How it works

Three simple steps from link to insight.

1

Paste a YouTube link

Drop in the URL of any public video with available captions or a transcript.

2

Choose your format

Pick a summary style — standard, detailed, code-focused, bullet points, or mind map — and your preferred output language.

3

Read your summary

Get an instant, structured summary along with sentiment insights, then revisit it anytime from your dashboard.
